Our Story

A family business rooted in community, culture, and the love of African food

"We opened African Food Market because we knew how hard it was to find the ingredients that make a house feel like home."

What started as a passion project has grown into one of Ottawa's most trusted African grocery stores, serving families from Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, the Congo, Senegal, and across the continent and diaspora.

We stock the real thing — authentic brands, real ingredients, no substitutes. Our shelves are restocked every week so what you find is always fresh. When a customer walks in looking for something specific, we do our best to source it.

Authenticity First

Every product is carefully sourced to match what you'd find back home — no imitations, no shortcuts.

Community Rooted

We're not just a store. We're a gathering point for Ottawa's African diaspora community.

Family Values

Run by family, for families. We treat every customer like a neighbour.
